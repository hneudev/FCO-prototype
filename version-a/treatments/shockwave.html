Focused Shockwave Therapy

Non-invasive treatment designed to stimulate a healing response.

This prototype page demonstrates how treatment information can be clear, visual and connected to the conditions, questions and aftercare patients care about.

Request an AppointmentAsk on WhatsApp

What it is

Focused acoustic energy, used with purpose.

Focused shockwave therapy uses targeted acoustic waves as a non-invasive treatment modality. On the production site, this section can explain the technology in plain language, supported by treatment-specific references and Courtney’s clinical explanation.

Why it belongs in the new structure

Explain the treatment without making the patient choose it.

The site can educate visitors while keeping the evaluation central. Treatment pages should help people understand options, not encourage self-diagnosis.

What a patient wants to know

Answer the practical questions first.

What does it feel like?

Use concise, reassuring language about the treatment experience and comfort.

How long is a session?

Give a realistic range and explain what can change depending on the treatment area.

How many sessions?

Explain that recommendations depend on the condition and individual plan.

Is there downtime?

Set expectations around activity, soreness and aftercare in a dedicated section.

What conditions use it?

Connect to relevant condition pages rather than listing disconnected keywords.

Is it right for me?

Keep the answer anchored to evaluation and individual clinical judgment.
