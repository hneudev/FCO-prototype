How Full Circle works

Care that starts with listening.

A simple, human process designed around understanding the problem before choosing the treatment.

01

We listen

Start with an unhurried conversation about your symptoms, history, goals and what you want to get back to doing.

02

We evaluate

Your provider reviews the full picture and looks for the root cause rather than treating symptoms in isolation.

03

We build your plan

Understand your options, expected process and what makes sense for your body, lifestyle and goals.

04

We stay connected

Ongoing communication and aftercare help you understand what comes next throughout your recovery.

Founder-led care

Meet Courtney Kelm, PA-C

Founder & Medical Director

Courtney founded Full Circle in 2019 around a simple idea: patients deserve time, honest answers and care built around their body. She brings more than 25 years of clinical experience, including adjunct faculty work at A.T. Still University and research coordination in oncology, neurology and rheumatology. She trained in Biomedical Sciences at Texas A&M University and completed her Physician Associate program at the University of Texas. She has been a guest speaker at the Barrow Neurological Institute and is active in international orthopedic societies.
