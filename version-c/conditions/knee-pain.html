Knee pain

Get back to moving without assuming surgery is the answer.

Knee pain can come from many different structures and conditions. The first step is understanding what is actually causing your symptoms and how they affect the way you move.

Common symptoms

What knee pain can feel like

This prototype demonstrates symptom-led navigation without asking the patient to diagnose themselves.

Pain on stairs

Discomfort when climbing, descending or getting up from a seated position.

Swelling

Visible or noticeable fullness, stiffness or pressure around the joint.

Clicking or catching

Popping, grinding or a sensation that the knee gets stuck during motion.

Activity pain

Pain after running, hiking, training or spending extended time on your feet.

Instability

A feeling that the knee may buckle, shift or not fully support you.

Loss of mobility

Difficulty fully bending or straightening the knee or returning to normal activity.

Possible causes

Your symptom is only the beginning of the story.

Knee pain can be associated with cartilage changes, meniscus issues, tendon injuries, ligament problems, overuse, arthritis and other causes. A useful evaluation looks at the whole picture instead of matching one symptom to one treatment.

The Full Circle approach

You don’t need to diagnose yourself.

The goal of the consultation is to understand your history, symptoms, movement and previous imaging or treatment, then discuss options in plain language.
